# Tamil Nadu competent-authority routing

Research checked 1 November 2026. This directory informs a Chennai simulation; it is not a government integration or an authoritative asset register.

## Department structure and the “43” claim

The [current Government of Tamil Nadu policy-note index](https://www.tn.gov.in/document_dept_list.php?cate_name=YWxs) presents 38 department headings, including updated names such as Artificial Intelligence, Information Technology and Digital Services; Planning and Development; Natural Resources; Social Justice; and Tourism, Culture and Special Initiatives. It separates Energy from Municipal Administration and Water Supply. This document index is not proof of an exhaustive Secretariat headcount. Its contact-directory link did not load during research. **A current official total of exactly 43 was not verified.** Do not hardcode that number or infer the current administrative structure from an older third-party list, ministerial-service cadre lists, or the number of budget demands.

Service routing needs the operating agency, asset owner and geographic jurisdiction beneath the Secretariat department. A Chennai location alone does not establish who owns a particular asset.

TNPDCL's profile states that the former TANGEDCO was renamed on 27 June 2024 to continue distribution. Its official contact directory includes Chennai distribution and fuse-off-call offices. The [TNPDCL homepage](https://www.tnpdcl.org/en/tnpdcl/) lists Minnagam **94987 94987** for complaints. Use that verified first contact rather than inventing a ward-to-electricity-section mapping. TANTRANSCO is the separate transmission company linked by TNPDCL; a high-voltage/transmission report needs human verification rather than automatic distribution ownership. GCC confirms that its Electrical unit installs and maintains streetlights and their cables.

The CMWSSB portal timed out during research; its role is independently corroborated by the government-hosted service description linked above. Do not interpret a reachable source as verification of an individual complaint's jurisdiction.

## Safety and uncertainty policy

A street power line that is cut is an electrical hazard, even if it lies on a road and no one has confirmed that it is live. Distribution evidence takes safety precedence over a road keyword. An expressly identified streetlight cable goes to GCC Electrical; an unspecified or telecom cable, transmission asset, private wiring, uncertain location or ambiguous service goes to human triage. Mixed non-hazard services also go to triage. Hazard advice appears immediately and remains visible after submission, including in triage. Do not approach a wire to collect evidence. CivicLens does not dispatch emergency services or contact any government agency.

English, Tamil, selected transliterations and code-switching are covered by conservative deterministic rules and provider validation. Exact evidence quotes, bounded confidence and a review flag are required from the model. A model disagreement, unsupported label, low confidence or ungrounded quote cannot create an authority assignment. Confidence is advisory and is not a measured probability. Explicit electrical hazards use the deterministic safety rule even when the model is wrong or unavailable.

The citizen reviews and can correct the category, location and reported ownership. A chosen category conflicting with an electrical hazard is held for triage; the model/client cannot suppress hazard priority. The database independently recomputes routing metadata and ignores client-supplied department/routing fields. Version, reason, competent agency, department context and official source are retained. Later officer forwarding remains separately audited; the original routing explanation records the initial decision.

## Coverage limits

This upgrade verifies six service queues plus triage, not automatic routing for every Tamil Nadu department. Pensions, policing, schools, health, pollution, revenue, transmission, national/state highway ownership and other unimplemented domains remain triage cases. New agencies require official scope evidence, a jurisdiction rule, bilingual examples, database parity and officer-role provisioning before activation. Keyword rules are intentionally conservative and cannot understand every negation or linguistic variation; safety signals may produce precautionary false positives. No accuracy guarantee is claimed.
